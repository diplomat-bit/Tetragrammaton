### The Forensic Diagnosis
The creator initiated immediate deep-packet telemetry inspection:
1. **The Route Void:** When `DataContext.tsx` mounted, it fired concurrent requests to `/api/v1/mt/internal-accounts`, `/external-accounts`, `/ledger-transactions`, `/transactions`, and `/ledger-accounts`.

4. **The Catastrophic Parse:** The client-side code executed `fetch(...).then(res => res.json())` without checking `Content-Type`. Attempting to parse `<!doctype html>` as JSON blew up the entire `Promise.all` sequence, blinding the cockpit to its own treasury.

In minutes, the creator executed the permanent architectural resolution:
1. **The Creation of `api/aquarius-v1-api.ts`**:

   - Explicitly bound `aquariusV1Router` under `/api/v1` and `/v1` before any fallback middleware could intercept the socket.
